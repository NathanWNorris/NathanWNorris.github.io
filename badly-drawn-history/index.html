Agent Hub – Badly Drawn History is Nathan Norris’s local workflow for researching, assembling, reviewing and scheduling videos for the Badly Drawn History YouTube channel.

This website explains the app and its Google connection. Channel connection and video controls are available inside the owner’s local Agent Hub application.

01 / MAKE

Build the episode

Research, scripts, narration, scene artwork, captions and rendering form the production workflow. Each provider requires its own authorized connection.

02 / REVIEW

Check the exact video

Technical checks and independent audiovisual review are required. The first two new production candidates also require the channel owner’s approval.

03 / SCHEDULE

Publish to the right channel

After Google consent, the app verifies the channel, uploads approved videos and checks the resulting video and scheduled publication time.

Why the app asks for Google access

The YouTube connection requests permission to upload videos and read YouTube account information. It uses this access to verify the owner’s channel, detect existing uploads and check video metadata, privacy, processing and scheduling status. See the privacy policy for data handling and revocation instructions.

What connection does—and does not—mean

The PC and required services must be running, approved providers must be available, and production and review requirements must pass. A website or saved OAuth client alone does not establish a working channel connection or guarantee an unattended episode. Google authorization can expire or be revoked.
