Purpose and access

Agent Hub – Badly Drawn History is a local production and publishing workflow operated by Nathan Norris for his authorized YouTube channel. This site provides information about the workflow; it does not offer public account registration, a paid service or a place to enter Google credentials. Google connection occurs inside Agent Hub through the owner’s consent.

Owner control and content

Only the channel owner or someone the owner explicitly authorizes may operate the connection. The operator is responsible for authorizing publication, reviewing required candidates and ensuring rights to videos, narration, artwork, music and other uploaded material. Connecting Google does not by itself approve a video or bypass a production or review requirement.

Availability

Operation depends on the owner’s PC, local app, internet connectivity, Google authorization, provider access and applicable usage limits. The app may hold work when a prerequisite is missing or an external outcome is uncertain. No uninterrupted operation, perpetual Google authorization or particular publication result is promised.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of those services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google data handling is described in this app’s privacy policy; Google’s own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at Google Account connections.
